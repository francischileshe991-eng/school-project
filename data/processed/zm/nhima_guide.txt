NATIONAL HEALTH INSURANCE MEDICAL SCHEME (NHIMA)
MEMBERSHIP REGISTRATION GUIDE
1.0 WHO CAN JOIN
NHIMA is open to all Zambian citizens and residents who:
- Are employed in the formal sector
- Are self-employed
- Are dependents of contributors
2.0 BENEFITS
Members enjoy the following benefits:
- Free medical treatment at accredited facilities
- Referral services to higher-level facilities
- Maternity services
- Emergency medical evacuation
