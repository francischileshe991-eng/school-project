# NHIMA Zambia Guide 2024
## 1. Contribution Rates
- **Formal Sector:** 1% from Employee, 1% from Employer (Total 2% of basic salary).
- **Informal Sector:** Average K50 per month for a family of up to 7 members.
- **Exemptions:** Senior citizens (65+), persons with disabilities, and the vulnerable (certified by Social Services).
## 2. Benefits Package
NHIMA provides "no-payment-at-point-of-service" for:
- Out-patient consultations.
- In-patient hospital stays and surgeries.
- Maternity and Neonatal care.
- Essential pharmaceuticals and drugs.
- Specialized services (Eye care, Dental, Physiotherapy).
## 3. Registration
- Register online at www.nhima.co.zm.
- Required: NRC number and scan of NRC.
- Beneficiaries: One spouse and up to 5 children under 18.
- Waiting Period: 4 months of contributions required before accessing services (unless a waiver is active).
