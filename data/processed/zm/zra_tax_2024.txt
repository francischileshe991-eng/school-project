# Zambia Revenue Authority (ZRA) Information 2024
## 1. PAYE Tax Thresholds (2024)
The monthly tax-free threshold for 2024 is K5,100.
| Monthly Income Range (ZMW) | Tax Rate |
| :--- | :--- |
| First K5,100 | 0% (Tax-Free) |
| Next K2,000 (K5,100.01 – K7,100) | 20% |
| Next K2,100 (K7,100.01 – K9,200) | 30% |
| Above K9,200 | 37% |
## 2. Taxpayer Identification Number (TPIN)
A TPIN is a unique 10-digit number required for all financial transactions in Zambia.
### Required Documents for TPIN
- **Zambian Citizens:** National Registration Card (NRC) and proof of residence.
- **Foreign Nationals:** Valid Passport and Immigration/Work Permit.
### Online Registration Steps
1. Visit the ZRA TaxOnline Portal (portal.zra.org.zm).
2. Click "Register" or "Get a TPIN".
3. Fill in the Individual or Business form.
4. Upload NRC and proof of residence.
5. Submit and wait for processing (usually within 24 hours).
