# Zimbabwe Health Guidance (Malaria & Cholera)

## 1. Cholera Prevention & Treatment
- **Safe Water:** Drink only boiled or treated water.
- **Hygiene:** Wash hands frequently with soap (Kugeza maoko nesipo).
- **Treatment:** Use Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS) and visit a clinic immediately if you have diarrhea.
- **Important:** Cholera remains an ongoing public health risk. Follow national alerts from the Ministry of Health and Child Care.

## 2. Malaria Prevention
- **Protection:** Sleep under mosquito nets (preferably insecticide-treated) and use repellents. Take chemoprophylaxis as advised if travelling to high-risk zones.
- **High Risk:** Zambezi Valley, Kariba, Victoria Falls and other low-lying areas (especially during the rainy season November to April).
- **Vaccination:** Malaria vaccines are available in Zimbabwe. Check eligibility and availability at local clinics.
- Shona keywords: Utano (Health), Chirwere (Disease), Malaria, Cholera.
