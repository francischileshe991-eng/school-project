# Zimbabwe Education & Results Guide (ZIMSEC & UZ)

## 1. How to Check ZIMSEC Results Online
1. Visit the portal at www.zimsec.co.zw and click "View Results."
2. Select your provincial portal (e.g., Harare, Bulawayo).
3. Register as a "New User" with your Center Number, Candidate Number, and Session (e.g., Nov 2024).
4. Log in to view and print your Grade 7, O-Level, or A-Level results.

## 2. University of Zimbabwe (UZ) Admissions
- **General Requirements:** Minimum 5 'O' Level passes (Grades A-C) including English Language.
- **Undergraduate:** At least 2 'A' Level passes are usually required.
- **Application Fee:** Approx USD 30 for local students.
- Shona keywords: Zvabuda (Results), Chikoro (School), Yunivhesiti (University).
