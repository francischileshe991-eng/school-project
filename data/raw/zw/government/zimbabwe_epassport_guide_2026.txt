# Civil Registry Department (Zimbabwe) - Official E-Passport & Identification Guide (2025/2026)

## 1. Overview of the Zimbabwean Biometric E-Passport
The Zimbabwean electronic passport (e-passport) is issued by the Civil Registry Department under the Ministry of Home Affairs and Cultural Heritage. It features an embedded microchip containing the bearer's biometric data (fingerprints and facial image) in compliance with international ICAO standards.

## 2. Schedule of E-Passport Fees (Statutory Instrument SI 2024-001)
- Ordinary E-Passport (processed within 7 to 14 working days): US$150.00.
- Emergency / Express E-Passport (processed within 48 to 72 hours): US$250.00.
- QR Code Surcharge / Digital Verification fee: US$20.00 (where applicable).
- Payment Methods: Payable in United States Dollars (USD) or prevailing official currency at designated CBZ Bank counters or online payment gateways.

## 3. Mandatory Requirements for Adults (Age 16 and Above)
1. Original and photocopies of the National Identity Card (metal card, plastic card, or green paper identity document with clear readable numbers).
2. Original long-form Birth Certificate and photocopy.
3. Two identical recent passport-sized color photographs (35mm x 45mm, white background, neutral facial expression, ears visible, no spectacles).
4. Previous passport (if applying for renewal or replacement).
5. Marriage certificate or official Gazette notice (for applicants who have changed names by marriage or deed poll).

## 4. Mandatory Requirements for Minors (Under Age 16)
1. Original long-form Birth Certificate of the child.
2. Original National Identity Cards of both parents.
3. Parental Consent: Both parents must be present in person during biometric capture. If one parent is living abroad or deceased, a signed sworn affidavit of consent or an official death certificate must be provided.
4. Court guardianship order if the child is in legal custody of a guardian.

## 5. Application and Biometric Enrollment Procedure
1. Online Pre-Enrolment: Complete the initial registration form on the official Civil Registry portal (https://www.rg.gov.zw).
2. Appointment Booking: Schedule a biometric appointment at your nearest provincial or district registry center (e.g., Makombe Building in Harare, Mhlahlandlela Government Complex in Bulawayo, Gweru, Mutare, Chitungwiza, or designated foreign embassies).
3. Biometric Enrollment: Present original documents, pay the fee, and capture digital fingerprints and facial photograph.
4. Passport Collection: The applicant is notified via SMS once the e-passport is printed and ready for collection.

## 6. Official Contact Channels
- Headquarters: Makombe Building, Corner Leopold Takawira & Herbert Chitepo Avenue, Harare, Zimbabwe.
