# Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA) - Tax Guide 2024 (Mutero we PAYE / Mari ya ZIMRA)

## 1. PAYE Tax Thresholds (USD - Foreign Currency)
- **Tax-Free Threshold:** Up to USD 400 per month.
- **USD 401 – USD 1,500:** 20%
- **USD 1,501 – USD 5,000:** 25%
- **USD 5,001 – USD 10,000:** 30%
- **USD 10,001 – USD 15,000:** 35%
- **Above USD 15,000:** 40%

## 2. PAYE Tax Thresholds (ZiG - Local Currency)
- **Tax-Free Threshold:** Up to ZiG 12,204.00 per month.
- **ZiG 12,204.01 – ZiG 36,612.00:** 20%
- **ZiG 36,612.01 – ZiG 122,040.00:** 25%
- **Above ZiG 366,120.01:** 40%

## 3. Aids Levy
A mandatory **3%** Aids Levy is added to the final calculated PAYE amount for all taxpayers.
