# Lusaka Water Supply and Sanitation Company (LWSC)
## Customer Service, Connection Requirements, and Sanitation Guidelines

### 1.0 About LWSC
Lusaka Water Supply and Sanitation Company (LWSC) is the licensed commercial utility responsible for the provision of potable piped water and sanitation services to the residents of Lusaka Province (Lusaka, Chongwe, Kafue, Luangwa, Chirundu, Rufunsa, and Shibuyunji districts), regulated by NWASCO.

### 2.0 Applying for a New Domestic Water Connection
To apply for a new individual domestic water connection with LWSC:
1. **Application Form:** Obtain and complete an Application for Water Supply and Sewerage Connection Form at any LWSC Branch or Peri-Urban Customer Service Centre.
2. **Mandatory Documentation:**
   - Certified copy of National Registration Card (NRC) or valid Passport.
   - Proof of property ownership (Title Deed, Land Record, Offer Letter from Lusaka City Council, or Chief's allocation letter).
   - Valid physical address / plot number / house number.
3. **Site Survey:** An LWSC technical inspector visits the premises within 48 hours to assess pipeline distance, pressure, and produce a formal estimate.
4. **Payment of Quotation:** Upon receiving the formal quotation, pay the connection fee at an authorized LWSC cashier or bank (ZNCB, Indo-Zambia, or mobile money).
5. **Meter Installation:** Once payment is verified, connection and water meter installation is executed within 7 to 14 working days.

### 3.0 Payment Channels & Bill Inquiries
- **Mobile Money:** Customers can pay water bills via MTN Mobile Money (*115#), Airtel Money (*778#), and Zamtel Kwacha (*344#) using their 8-digit LWSC Account Number.
- **Commercial Banks:** Lusaka Water accounts are payable through Zanaco BillPay, Indo-Zambia Bank, and Atlas Mara.
- **E-Portal:** Account balance inquiries and e-statements are accessible at www.lwsc.com.zm.

### 4.0 Sewerage & Pit Latrine Emptying Services
- LWSC operates wastewater treatment plants in Manchinchi and Chunga.
- Peri-urban vacuum tanker faecal sludge emptying services can be requested through peri-urban offices at standardized NWASCO-subsidized rates.
