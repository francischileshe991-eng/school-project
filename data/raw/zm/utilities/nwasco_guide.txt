# NWASCO Water Connection Guide (Zambia) - [Madzi pakhomo / Ukututila amenshi]

## 1. Mandatory Requirements for New Connection (Zofunika / Ififwaikwa)
To apply for a water connection (Ndingatenge bwanji madzi), you need:
- **Identification:** Copy of National Registration Card (NRC) or Passport.
- **Ownership Proof:** Copy of Title Deed, Land Record, or Council Offer Letter.
- **Application:** Completed Account Opening Form from the utility.

## 2. Connection Process
1. **Application:** Submit documents at a customer service center.
2. **Site Survey:** Utility conducts a survey within 48 hours to estimate costs.
3. **Payment:** Pay the connection fee and refundable security deposit.
4. **Installation:** Utility installs the meter and connects service (usually 7-14 days).

## 3. Consumer Rights (NWASCO Regulations)
- Utilities must provide a site survey within 48 hours.
- Connection fees should be standard and approved by NWASCO.
- Consumers can request payment plans for connection fees.
- Labor for standard connections is typically included in the connection fee.
