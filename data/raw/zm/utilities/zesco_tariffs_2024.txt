# ZESCO Electricity Tariffs Zambia (2026 Multi-Year Framework) - [Ama units / Magesi]

## 1. RESIDENTIAL TARIFFS 2026 (Mitengo ya magesi / Unit nishi)
- **Band 1 (Lifeline):** 0 - 100 kWh costs **K0.42 per unit**.
- **Band 2:** 101 - 200 kWh costs **K1.19 per unit**.
- **Band 3:** 201 - 400 kWh costs **K2.89 per unit**.
- **Band 4:** Above 400 kWh costs **K4.12 per unit**.

## 2. OFFICIAL POLICY
- The 2024 Emergency Drought Tariffs (K6.39) were officially REMOVED on Oct 31, 2025.
- Fixed monthly charges have been eliminated for all residential households.
