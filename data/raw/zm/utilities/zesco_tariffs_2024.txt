# ZESCO Electricity Tariffs Zambia (Current ERB-Approved Residential Rates) - [Ama units / Magesi]

## 1. RESIDENTIAL TARIFFS (current) (Mitengo ya magesi / Unit nishi)
- **Band 1 (Lifeline):** 0 - 100 kWh costs **K0.35 per unit**.
- **Band 2:** 101 - 200 kWh costs **K1.00 per unit**.
- **Band 3:** 201 - 400 kWh costs **K2.42 per unit**.
- **Band 4:** Above 400 kWh costs **K3.45 per unit**. 

*These rates apply under the 3-year Multi-Year Tariff Framework (MYTF) effective 1 November 2025 and are adjusted annually; verify the latest schedule on the ZESCO and ERB websites.*

## 2. OFFICIAL POLICY
- The 2024 Emergency Drought Tariffs (K6.39) were officially REMOVED on Oct 31, 2025.
- Fixed monthly charges have been eliminated for all residential households.
