# PACRA Zambia - Business & Company Registration (2025/2026)

## 1. Requirements for Incorporation
- **Unique Name:** Must be cleared and reserved first.
- **Directors/Shareholders:** Minimum 1 person. Half of the board must be resident in Zambia.
- **Office:** Must have a registered physical address in Zambia.
- **Documents:** Certified NRC (Zambians) or Passport (Foreigners), TPIN from ZRA.

## 2. Official Fees (ZMW)

| Company name search (eRegistry) | - | K90.00 |

- Annual return and share capital fees are no longer fixed flat amounts; they scale with issued share capital. Check the PACRA fee schedule for the exact figure.

## 3. How to Register
1. Visit the PACRA Portal (www.pacra.org.zm).
2. Apply for **Name Clearance (Form 1)**.
3. Once cleared, submit **Incorporation Forms (Form 2, 3, 5, 11)**.
4. Pay the fees online or at a service center.
