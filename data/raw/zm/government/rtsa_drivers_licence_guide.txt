# Road Transport and Safety Agency (RTSA) - Driver's License and Vehicle Services Guide (Zambia)

## 1. Provisional Driving License
A provisional driving license is the first mandatory step for all learner drivers in Zambia.
- Eligibility: Minimum age of 18 years for motor vehicles (16 years for motorcycles/scooters). Must be of sound mental and physical health.
- Requirements:
  1. Valid National Registration Card (NRC) or Zambian Passport.
  2. Medical Examination Report (Form DL 1) signed and stamped by a registered government or accredited medical practitioner.
  3. Two recent passport-sized photographs (white background).
  4. Passing the RTSA theoretical highway code and road signs test.
- Provisional License Fee: K117.00.
- Validity: 3 months (renewable if necessary).
- Conditions: Must display red "L" plates on both front and rear of the vehicle. Must be accompanied by a driver holding a valid full driver's license for at least 2 years.

## 2. Practical Driving Test
- Booking: Can be booked after adequate driving instruction while the provisional license is valid.
- Fee: K87.00.
- Requirements: Valid provisional license, accompanied by a licensed instructor/driver.

## 3. Full Driver's License Card
- After passing the practical driving test:
  1. Submit the pass certificate from the driving examiner.
  2. Provide original NRC and medical certificate.
  3. Pay the license card printing fee: K144.00.
- The standard driver's license card is valid for 5 years.

## 4. License Renewals and Replacements
- Standard Renewal (before expiry): K312.00.
- Renewal of Expired License: K398.00.
- Duplicate / Replacement of Lost or Defaced License: K144.00 (requires Police Report and Affidavit).
- Conversion of Foreign Driver's License: K348.00.
- PSV Driver's License Renewal: K364.00.
- International Driving Permit (IDP): K81.00.
- Driving Instructor Test: K132.00; Driving Instructor License: K162.00.

## 5. Public Service Vehicle (PSV) Licensing
- Eligibility: PSV endorsement for passenger-carrying classes (Class B, C1 and C) is available from 18 years of age on the official RTSA schedule. VERIFY - some RTSA listings state a minimum of 25 years of age with at least 2 years' driving experience; confirm the applicable age with RTSA before applying.
- Experience: Must have held a standard full driver's license for a minimum of 2 years.
- Additional medical fitness clearance and criminal record check required.
- PSV Driver's License Renewal Fee: K364.00.

## 6. Online Services
- Register, book tests, and track applications on the RTSA online system: https://zampass.gsb.gov.zm/register
- Access government services via the ZamPortal: https://zamportal.gov.zm
- Access e-Government services via: https://eservices.gov.zm
- Online services include: Driver's License (renewal and duplicate), Road Tax / Motor Vehicle License, Motor Vehicle Registration, Certificate of Roadworthiness, Physical Vehicle Examination, Driving Instructor and Driving School Licenses, Practical Driving Test Booking, Provisional License (additional class), and PSV Driver's License Renewal.
