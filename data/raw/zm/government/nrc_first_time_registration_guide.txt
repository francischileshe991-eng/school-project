# Zambia National Registration Card (NRC) - First-Time Applicant & Registration Guide
# (Ukupoka NRC iya Mupando lwa Kubalilapo / Kupanga NRC Yatsopano Yoyambirira)
# Republic of Zambia — Department of National Registration, Passport and Citizenship (DNRPC)
# Ministry of Home Affairs and Internal Security | National Registration Act (Chapter 126 of the Laws of Zambia)

## 1. Overview & Statutory Mandate
The National Registration Card (commonly known as the "Green NRC" or "Icishibilo ca Mupando") is the official statutory primary identity document for citizens of the Republic of Zambia. 
- **Governing Law:** National Registration Act, Chapter 126 of the Laws of Zambia.
- **Issuing Department:** Department of National Registration, Passport and Citizenship (DNRPC), Ministry of Home Affairs and Internal Security.
- **Legal Mandate:** Under Section 8 of the National Registration Act, every Zambian citizen who has attained the age of 16 years is statutorily mandated to register and obtain a National Registration Card.

## 2. Official Registration Fee
- **Statutory Fee:** **STRICTLY FREE OF CHARGE (K0.00)**.
- First-time registration for a Zambian Green NRC is an inalienable statutory civic right. It is completely free of charge.
- It is illegal and a criminal offense under Zambian law for any registration officer, agent, or intermediary to charge or solicit money for first-time registration.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria for First-Time Registration
1. **Age Requirement:** The applicant must be a Zambian citizen who has attained the age of 16 years or older.
2. **Citizenship:** Must be a citizen of Zambia by birth, by descent, or by legal registration under the Constitution of Zambia.
3. **Registration Status:** The applicant must never have been issued an NRC before (persons replacing a lost or damaged card must follow the replacement procedure).

## 4. Required Supporting Documents for First-Time Applicants
To register for a new Green NRC, the applicant must present verifiable proof of birth and proof of parentage:

### A. Proof of Age & Birth (Any one of the following):
1. **Official Full Birth Certificate** issued by the Registrar General of Births, Marriages, and Deaths under the Births and Deaths Registration Act (Cap 51); OR
2. **Under-Five Health Card (Clinic Baby Hospital Card / Road to Health Card)** showing verified date of birth, place of birth, and parents' particulars; OR
3. **Certified Baptismal Certificate** showing birth date and parent details, accompanied by an affidavit of birth.

### B. Proof of Parentage & Zambian Citizenship:
1. **Parent Accompaniment:** The applicant must be accompanied physically in person by at least one biological parent (father or mother) who is a Zambian citizen holding a valid original Green National Registration Card (NRC).
2. **Deceased Parents:** If one or both parents are deceased:
   - Certified Death Certificates or Burial Permits of the deceased parent(s).
   - Physical accompaniment by a recognized legal guardian or close blood relative (uncle, aunt, or grandparent) holding a valid original Green NRC.
3. **Rural & Traditional Community Residents:**
   - In rural areas or communities without formal clinical records, an introductory confirmation letter signed and stamped by the recognized Traditional Chief, Village Headman, or Ward Councillor certifying the applicant's identity, family lineage, village of origin, and parentage.

### C. Official Application Form:
- The applicant will complete **Form A** (*Application for Registration of a Citizen of Zambia*), provided free of charge at the registration office.

## 5. Step-by-Step First-Time Registration Procedure
Applying for a first-time Green NRC involves four straightforward steps:

- **Step 1: Document Submission and Verification:**
  - Visit the nearest District National Registration Office (Boma / Civic Centre) in your home district.
  - Present your proof of birth (Birth Certificate or Under-Five Card) and your accompanying parent's original Green NRC to the registration desk.
  - Officers verify the parent's NRC on the national register and confirm eligibility.

- **Step 2: Completion of Registration Form A:**
  - Complete Form A with the applicant's full statutory name, date of birth, sex, place of birth, village, Chief, district of registration, and parents' registration numbers.
  - The accompanying parent or guardian signs the declaration confirming parentage.

- **Step 3: In-Person Biometric Enrollment:**
  - The applicant must appear physically in person for live biometric capture.
  - Live electronic rolled and plain ten-fingerprint impression capture.
  - High-resolution facial portrait photograph capture taken directly at the DNRPC biometric booth.

- **Step 4: Card Printing, Lamination, and Issuance:**
  - The registration card (Green NRC / Form B) is printed containing the citizen's unique 9-digit registration number (e.g., 123456/11/1).
  - The card is signed by the District Registrar and the citizen, laminated in official tamper-evident security plastic, and issued immediately to the applicant.

## 6. Where to Apply (Registration Centres)
- **District National Registration Offices:** Located at District Administration Boma offices and Civic Centres in all 116 districts across Zambia.
- **Provincial Headquarters:** DNRPC Provincial Offices located in Lusaka, Ndola, Kitwe, Livingstone, Choma, Chipata, Kasama, Mansa, Solwezi, Kabwe, and Mongu.
- **Mobile Registration Exercises:** Periodic mobile registration outreach centres deployed to schools, rural wards, and community centres.

## 7. Multilingual Reference & Community Vocabulary
- **icibemba (Bemba):**
  - Ukupoka NRC iya mupando lwa kubalilapo (Obtaining an NRC for the first time).
  - Icishibilo ca bwana calo ica Green (Green citizen identity card).
  - Kuti mwapoka fye pa fye ukwabula ukulipila nangu kamo (Strictly free of charge).
  - Mufwile ukuba ne myaka ikumi na mutanda (16 years) nangu ukucilapo.
  - Mufwile ukuya no mufyashi uukwete NRC iya Green, nangu kalata ya kwa Mfumu nangu Headman.
- **chiNyanja (Nyanja):**
  - Kupanga NRC yatsopano yoyambirira (Making a new NRC for the first time).
  - Kulembetsa kwa nzika ya Zambia yomwe yafika zaka 16 zakubadwa.
  - Ntchito imeneyi ndi yaulere ndithu, ndizoletsedwa kulipira ndalama iliyonse (Strictly free of charge).
  - Muyenera kubwera ndi kholo lomwe lili ndi Green NRC, kapena kalata yochokera kwa Mfumu kapena Mwinimuzi.
