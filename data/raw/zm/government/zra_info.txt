ZAMBIA REVENUE AUTHORITY (ZRA)
TAX INFORMATION GUIDE

1.0 INTRODUCTION
The Zambia Revenue Authority (ZRA) is a statutory body established under the Zambia Revenue Authority Act No. 5 of 2009. ZRA is responsible for the assessment, collection and accounting for revenue on behalf of the Government of the Republic of Zambia.

2.0 TAX TYPES
ZRA administers the following taxes:
- Income Tax
- Value Added Tax (VAT)
- Customs Duty
- Excise Duty
- Pay As You Earn (PAYE)

3.0 TAX REGISTRATION
All taxpayers are required to register with ZRA before commencing business operations.
