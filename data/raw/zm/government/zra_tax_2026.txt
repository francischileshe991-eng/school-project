# Zambia Revenue Authority (ZRA) - Official Tax Guide (2025/2026)

## 1. Pay As You Earn (PAYE) Individual Tax Bands
Zambia operates a progressive monthly PAYE tax system for all formally employed workers:
- First Band (Tax-Free Threshold): K0.00 to K5,100.00 per month is taxed at 0% (Tax-free).
- Second Band: K5,100.01 to K7,100.00 per month is taxed at 20%.
- Third Band: K7,100.01 to K9,200.00 per month is taxed at 30%.
- Fourth Band: Above K9,200.00 per month is taxed at 37%.

*Note: The tax is progressive; each rate only applies to income falling within that specific monthly bracket.*

## 2. Taxpayer Identification Number (TPIN) Registration
A TPIN is a mandatory 10-digit number required for all tax and commercial transactions in Zambia, including opening bank accounts, motor vehicle registration, and clearing goods.
- Procedure:
  1. Go to the ZRA TaxOnline portal at https://portal.zra.org.zm/ or use the mobile TaxOnApp.
  2. Select "Register TPIN" and choose your taxpayer category (Individual or Corporate/Business).
  3. Enter personal details: full names as they appear on your NRC, date of birth, physical residential address, and phone number.
  4. Upload a scanned copy of your National Registration Card (NRC) or passport.
  5. Submit the application. Upon approval, you will receive an SMS and email notification with your 10-digit TPIN certificate, which you can print online immediately.
- Registration Fee: Free of charge.

## 3. Turnover Tax for Small and Medium Businesses
- Eligibility: Micro and small businesses with annual gross turnover up to K5,000,000 (K5 million). Businesses whose annual turnover exceeds K5,000,000 must register for Income Tax instead.
- Rate (2026): 0% on annual turnover up to K30,000 (equivalent to monthly turnover of K2,500 or less); 5% on annual turnover above K30,000 up to K5,000,000.
- Basis: Turnover Tax is charged on gross sales/turnover (not on profit).
- Due Date: Returns and payments are due by the 14th day of the following month.
- Exclusions: Partnerships, businesses providing management/consultancy services, mining operations (other than artisanal and small-scale miners), and businesses registered for VAT cannot register for Turnover Tax.
- Note: The turnover ceiling was raised from K800,000 to K5,000,000 and the rate restructured from a flat 4% to 0%/5% effective 1 January 2025 (Income Tax (Amendment) Act No. 22 of 2024). The K800,000 figure is the VAT registration threshold, not the Turnover Tax ceiling.

## 4. Value Added Tax (VAT)
- Standard VAT Rate: 16% on taxable goods and services.
- Compulsory VAT Registration Threshold: Annual taxable turnover exceeding K800,000.
- Essential medical supplies, basic foodstuffs, and agricultural products are exempt or zero-rated.
