# Patents and Companies Registration Agency (PACRA) - Business Registration Guide (2025/2026)

## 1. Overview and Digital Mandate
PACRA is the statutory authority in Zambia responsible for the registration of companies, business names, and intellectual property.
- Digital Certificates: All certificates of incorporation and business registration are now issued in secure digital PDF format directly to the applicant's registered email address. Physical parchment certificates have been phased out.

## 2. Registering a Business Name (Sole Proprietorship / Partnership)
A Business Name registration is ideal for individual traders, consultants, and small partnership enterprises.
- Procedure:
  1. Name Search and Clearance: Propose up to three names online at https://www.onlinepacra.org.zm or via ZamPortal (www.gsb.gov.zm). Fee: K120.00.
  2. Completion of Form BN 3: Provide business physical address, principal line of business, and personal details of proprietor(s).
  3. Attach scanned copy of National Registration Card (NRC) or passport.
  4. Pay registration fee: typically K166.00 to K222.40.
  5. Receive electronic Certificate of Registration with a verifiable QR code.

## 3. Registering a Private Company Limited by Shares (Ltd)
- Key Requirements:
  - Minimum of two (2) directors and one (1) shareholder.
  - At least half of the directors must be ordinarily resident in Zambia.
  - Directors must be at least 18 years old and of sound mind.
  - Clear NRC copies for all Zambian directors/shareholders or valid passports for foreign directors.
  - Proposed registered office physical address in Zambia.
- Fee Structure: Scaled according to nominal share capital (minimum standard capital incorporation fees apply).
- Payment methods: Mobile Money (MTN, Airtel) and Visa/Mastercard debit cards.

## 4. Annual Returns Compliance
- Every registered business and company in Zambia is legally required to file Annual Returns with PACRA every calendar year.
- Filing keeps the company in "Good Standing" and prevents deregistration or punitive financial penalties.
