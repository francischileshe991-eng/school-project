# NAPSA Zambia - Pension & Contributions Guide (2025/2026)

## 1. Contribution Rates
The mandatory contribution rate is **10% of gross earnings**.
- **Employee:** 5% of gross pay.
- **Employer:** 5% of the employee's gross pay.
- Mandatory membership now also covers the **informal sector, self-employed and domestic workers** (NAPSA Act No. 72 of 2026, which replaced the 1996 Act).

## 2. Contribution Ceilings (Maximum)
- **2024 Ceiling:** Max monthly earnings is K29,816.00 (Max contribution K1,490.80).
- **2025 Ceiling:** Max monthly earnings is **K34,164.00** (Max contribution **K1,708.20**).
- **2026 Ceiling:** Max monthly earnings is **K37,236.00** (Max contribution **K1,861.80 per side; K3,723.60 combined employee + employer**).

## 3. Retirement Age
- **Early Retirement:** 55 years.
- **Normal Retirement:** 60 years.
- **Late Retirement:** 65 years.

## 4. How to Check Balance
- **Online:** Log in at www.napsa.co.zm using your Social Security Number.
- **Mobile:** Download the "NAPSA Mobile" app.
- **USSD:** Dial **\*677#** on your registered phone.
