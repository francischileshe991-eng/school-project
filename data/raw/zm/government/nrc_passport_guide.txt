# Zambia National Registration & Passport Guide (Icishibilo ca NRC/Capa/Mupando)

## 1. National Registration Card (NRC - Icishibilo)
- **Age Requirement:** 16 years or older (Imyaka 16).
- **First-time Applicants:** Free for citizens.
- **Replacement (Kusintha NRC/Ngatadaya):** If you lose or damage your NRC, a replacement costs **K3.10** (Lost) or **K2.20** (Damaged).

## 2. Passport Application
- **Process:** Apply at the Department of National Registration, Passport and Citizenship or via the Zambia E-Services Portal (www.gsb.gov.zm).
- **Requirements:** Copy of NRC, Birth Certificate, and two passport-sized photos.
- **Cost (Standard 32-Page):** K320 total.
- **Cost (Standard 48-Page):** K520 total.
