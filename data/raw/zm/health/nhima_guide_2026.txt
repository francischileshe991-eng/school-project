# National Health Insurance Management Authority (NHIMA) - Benefits & Registration Guide (2025/2026)

## 1. Scheme Mandate and Contribution Model
NHIMA manages the national social health insurance scheme established under the National Health Insurance Act to provide accessible, affordable, and quality healthcare for all Zambians.
- Contribution Rates:
  - Formal Employees: 1% deducted from monthly basic salary.
  - Employers: 1% matching contribution on the employee's basic salary (2% total).
  - No Salary Ceiling: Contributions apply to the entire basic salary.
  - Informal / Self-Employed Members: Contribute 1% of assessed monthly income through the eNHIMA system.
- Compulsory Membership: All Zambian citizens and established residents aged 18 to 65 with an income are required to contribute to NHIMA. Citizens aged 65 and above, and qualifying poor or vulnerable persons, are registered without making the ordinary contribution.
- Payment Deadline: Contributions are due on or before the 10th day of the following month.

## 2. Benefits and Medical Package
Registered members with up-to-date contributions are covered across accredited public and private healthcare facilities nationwide:
- Primary Consultations: Inpatient and outpatient consultations with general practitioners and clinical officers.
- Specialist Referrals: Consultations with specialized physicians, surgeons, pediatricians, and gynecologists.
- Diagnostic Investigations: Full laboratory blood work, X-rays, ultrasound scans, CT scans, and MRI investigations.
- Maternity and Child Health: Antenatal care, safe deliveries, cesarean sections, postnatal care, and newborn monitoring.
- Surgeries and Inpatient Admissions: Standard ward admission bed fees, anesthesia, surgical procedures, and in-hospital medications.
- Essential Drugs: Prescribed medications listed on the National Health Insurance Essential Drug Formulary.

## 3. Registration and Activation
- Online Registration: Open an account on the eNHIMA portal at https://enhima.nhima.co.zm.
- Requirements: Scanned copy of National Registration Card (NRC), phone number, and employer registration code (if formally employed).
- In-Person Registration: Visit the designated NHIMA customer desk at accredited hospitals (e.g., UTH, Levy Mwanawasa University Teaching Hospital, Ndola Teaching Hospital, Kitwe Teaching Hospital).
- Employer Obligation: Employers must register their employees with NHIMA and begin remitting contributions within 30 days.
- Waiting Period: VERIFY - the four (4) month consecutive-contribution waiting period for informal members before elective care activates could not be confirmed against current NHIMA guidance; confirm with NHIMA before relying on it (emergency stabilization is always covered).
