# NHIMA Zambia Official Guide (2026)

## 1. CONTRIBUTION COSTS (How much it costs)
As of May 2026, the contribution rules are:
- **Formal Sector Employees:** 1% of your **TOTAL GROSS MONTHLY SALARY**.
- **Employers:** 1% of the employee's gross monthly salary.
- **Total:** 2% of gross pay.
- **NO SALARY CEILING:** You pay 1% on your entire salary, even if it is very high.

## 2. INFORMAL SECTOR
- Individuals and self-employed: Average **K50 per month** for a family of 7.
- Senior Citizens (65+): Exempt/Free.

## 3. BENEFITS PACKAGE (Mapindu / Ifisuma)
NHIMA members enjoy:
- **Out-patient and In-patient** hospital care at accredited facilities.
- **Maternity & Neonatal Care (Ifumo / Amayi oyembekezera):** Includes delivery (normal or C-section) and postnatal care.
- **Pharmacy:** Essential drugs and medicines.
- **Diagnostics:** X-rays, scans, and laboratory tests.
- **Specialized Services:** Eye care, dental, and physiotherapy.
