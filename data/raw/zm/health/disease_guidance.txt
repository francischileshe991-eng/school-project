# Common Disease Guidance (Zambia)

## 1. Malaria
- **Prevention:** Sleep under Insecticide-Treated Nets (ITNs), use mosquito repellent, and allow Indoor Residual Spraying (IRS) in your home.
- **Treatment:** If you have fever or chills, get tested immediately (RDT test). Standard treatment in Zambia is ACT (Artemisinin-based Combination Therapy).

## 2. Cholera
- **Prevention:** Drink boiled or chlorinated water. Wash hands with soap after using the toilet and before handling food. Eat well-cooked, hot food.
- **Treatment:** If you have sudden diarrhea, start Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS) or Salt and Sugar Solution (SSS) immediately. Report to the nearest health facility or Cholera Treatment Center (CTC) without delay.
