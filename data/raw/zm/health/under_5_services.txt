# Zambia Under-5 Health Services Guide (2026)

This guide covers the free healthcare services provided by the Ministry of Health for children from birth until their fifth birthday.

## 1. The Under-5 Clinic Card (Khadi lachipatala la mwana / Icitabo ca mwana)
The "Under-5 Card" is a free health passport provided to all children. It tracks growth, vaccinations, and development.
- **Nyanja:** Khadi lachipatala la mwana ndi laulere ndipo limagwiritsidwa ntchito poyang'anira kukula ndi katemera wa mwana mpaka zaka zisanu.
- **Bemba:** Icitabo ca mwana caba fye caulere kabili cilafwa ukwishiba ifyo umwana alekula na mafunde ya magesi (katemera) yonse mpaka imyaka isano.

## 2. Vaccination Schedule (Routine Immunization)
Zambia protects children against major preventable diseases.
- **At Birth:** BCG (Tuberculosis), OPV 0 (Polio).
- **6, 10, & 14 Weeks:** Polio, Diphtheria, Pertussis, Tetanus, Hep B, Hib, Pneumonia, Rotavirus (Diarrhea).
- **9 Months:** Measles-Rubella 1, Vitamin A.
- **Malaria Vaccine (R21/Matrix-M):** Launched in Zambia on 27 October 2025. Four doses given at 6, 7, 8 and 18-23 months of age. Introduced in a phased manner, starting in 83 high-burden districts, alongside bed nets and other malaria control measures.
- **HPV Vaccine:** Given to girls aged 9-14 years to protect against cervical cancer.
- **18 Months:** Measles-Rubella 2 (Booster).
- **Nyanja:** Katemera amaperekedwa pa kubadwa, pa milungu 6, 10, 14, ndiponso pa miyezi 9 ndi 18 kuti ateteze mwana ku matenda oopsa.
- **Bemba:** Umwana alapeelwa katemera pa kufyalwa, pa milungu 6, 10, 14, na pa myeshi 9 na 18 pa kuti acingililwe ku malwele ayakalamba.

## 3. Services Offered (Mapindu / Ifisuma)
- **Growth Monitoring:** Regular weighing and height checks.
- **Nutritional Advice:** Guidance on breastfeeding and weaning.
- **Supplements:** Vitamin A and deworming tablets every 6 months.
- **Nyanja:** Amayeza kulemera kwa mwana, kupereka malangizo a kadyedwe, ndiponso kupereka mavitamini ndi mankhwala a njoka m'mimba.
- **Bemba:** Balapima umwana ulemu, balapeela amano pa fyakulya, kabili balapeela amavitamini ne mpulungushwa sha mu nda.

---
## Requirements for Parents
Always bring the Under-5 Card to the clinic for every visit. If born at home, visit the nearest clinic within 13 days to receive your card.
