# Technical Education, Vocational and Entrepreneurship Training Authority (TEVETA) Zambia
## Vocational Courses, Trade Tests, and Admission Guidelines (2025/2026)

### 1.0 Overview of TEVETA
TEVETA is the regulatory body established under the Technical Education, Vocational and Entrepreneurship Training Act No. 13 of 1998 to regulate, coordinate, and monitor technical education and vocational training in Zambia.

### 2.0 Key Levels of Qualifications
TEVETA accredits programs across four primary training tiers:
1. **Trade Test Level:** Level III (Basic), Level II (Intermediate), Level I (Advanced Craft). Designed for practical skills and open to candidates with Grade 7, Grade 9, or Grade 12 certificates.
2. **Craft Certificate:** 2-year practical training programs for Grade 12 school leavers with passes in Mathematics, Science, and English.
3. **Technician / Diploma Level:** 3-year advanced technical training programs requiring a minimum of 5 'O' Level credits including Mathematics, Science, and English Language.
4. **Skills Awards & Short Courses:** 3 to 6-month specialized entrepreneurship and competence-based certificates.

### 3.0 Popular TEVETA Accredited Courses
- **Engineering & Technical Fields:**
  - Automotive Mechanics and Heavy Equipment Repair
  - Electrical Engineering and Installation
  - Metal Fabrication and Welding
  - Mechanical Engineering and Fitting
  - Civil Engineering and Building Construction Technology
- **Information Technology & Media:**
  - Information Technology (IT) and Computer Systems
  - Telecommunications Technology
  - Graphic Design and Journalism
- **Business, Hospitality & Trades:**
  - Food Production, Hospitality Management, and Catering
  - Fashion Design and Tailoring
  - Cosmetology and Hairdressing
  - Business Administration and Purchasing & Supply

### 4.0 Major Public TEVETA Training Institutions in Zambia
- **Lusaka Province:** Evelyn Hone College of Applied Arts and Commerce, Lusaka Vocational Training Centre (LVTC), National Institute of Public Administration (NIPA).
- **Copperbelt Province:** Northern Technical College (NORTEC - Ndola), Luanshya Technical and Business College (LTBC), Copperbelt University Technical Campus.
- **Central Province:** Kabwe Institute of Technology (KIT).
- **Southern Province:** Livingstone Institute of Business and Engineering Studies (LIBES), Choma Trades Training Institute.
- **Eastern Province:** Chipata Trades Training Institute.
- **Northern / Luapula Provinces:** Lukashya Trades Training Institute (Kasama), Mansa Trades Training Institute.

### 5.0 Entry Requirements & Application Procedure
- **Craft and Diploma Programs:** Minimum five (5) O-Level credits including English Language and Mathematics.
- **Trade Testing:** Open to school leavers and informal apprentices seeking formal trade accreditation through practical assessment.
- **Application Portal:** Apply directly to the accredited college or visit the TEVETA portal at www.teveta.org.zm.
- **TEVETA Bursary:** Needy students enrolled in registered technical and trade institutes can apply for TEVETA bursary support through the Skills Development Fund (SDF).
