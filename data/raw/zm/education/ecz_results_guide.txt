# Examination Council of Zambia (ECZ) - Results Guide

## 1. How to Check Results / Get a Results Slip Online
1. Visit the ECZ Examination Results Slip Service at **estatements.exams-council.org.zm:8000**.
2. Select the Exam Level (Grade 7, 9, or 12 / GCE).
3. Select the Examination Year.
4. Enter your Examination Number and click Search.
5. The full result details are displayed; click **Generate Slip** to download an official PDF results slip. Enable pop-ups in your browser if the slip does not appear.
- For online confirmation of results, use the Online Results Confirmation System (ORCS) at **verify.exams-council.org.zm**.
- Other ECZ portals: **serviceportal.exams-council.org.zm** (services) and **systems.exams-council.org.zm** (candidate registration).

## 2. How to Check Results via SMS
1. Open your SMS app (MTN, ZAMTEL or AIRTEL).
2. Type: [Examination Number] [Year] [Grade].
   Example: 230123456789 2023 G12
3. Send the message to **8383**.
4. A service fee (approx. K4 - K5) will be deducted from your airtime.

## 3. Collecting Original Results
Candidates should collect original transcripts/results from the **school or examination centre** where they wrote, not from ECZ offices.
