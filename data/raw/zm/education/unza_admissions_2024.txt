# University of Zambia (UNZA) Admissions 2024

## 1. General Admission Requirements
- Minimum of five (5) O-Level credits (Grades 1-6).
- Mandatory: English Language.
- Science programs require Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, and Physics.

## 2. Popular Schools
- **School of Medicine:** High requirements (English, Math, Sciences).
- **School of Engineering:** Requires English, Math, and Physics.
- **School of Law:** Requires five credits including English.
- **Humanities & Social Sciences:** Requires five credits including English.

## 3. Application Process
- Apply online at oap.unza.ac.zm.
- Application Fee: K150 (Zambian/SADC) or USD 60 (International).
- Alternative Entry: A-Levels (Direct to 2nd year) or Diplomas (Direct to 2nd/3rd year).
