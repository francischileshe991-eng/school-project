# University of Zambia (UNZA) Admissions Guide (2026/2027)

## 1. General Admission Requirements
- Minimum of five (5) O-Level credits (Grades 1-6).
- Mandatory: English Language.
- Science/STEM programs require Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, and Physics.

## 2. 2026 and 2027 Intake Context
- **2026 Intake:** Applications closed on **17 April 2026**.
- **2027 Intake:** UNZA has published a call for undergraduate admission applications for the 2027 academic year; apply online at **oap.unza.ac.zm** or collect the application form from the university.
- **Application Fee:** **K150** (Zambian/SADC) or **USD 60** (International), non-refundable.
- **Website:** www.unza.ac.zm

## 3. Alternative Entry
- Direct entry to 2nd year available for A-Level holders (Grade C+ in Math/Physics) or relevant Diplomas.
