# Public Universities in Zambia - Application Guide (2025/2026)

This guide covers application procedures for major public universities.

## 1. Copperbelt University (CBU)
- **Website:** www.cbu.ac.zm
- **Nyanja:** Kuti mulembetse ku CBU, pitani pa portal (cbu.ac.zm/opus). Pangani akaunti, ikani NRC yanu ndi ma results. Lipirani K200 ku ZANACO.
- **Bemba:** Pa kulembesha ku CBU, kabiyeni pa portal (cbu.ac.zm/opus). Pangani akaunti, bikenipo NRC yanu na ma results. Lipileni K200 ku ZANACO.
- **Requirements:** 5 O-level credits including English and Math.

## 2. Mulungushi University (MU)
- **Website:** www.mu.ac.zm
- **Application fee (2026/2027 intake):** **K250** for Zambians (US$50 for non-Zambians), non-refundable, deposited into the Mulungushi University ZANACO account **1067654300160**.
- **Nyanja:** Lembetsani pa student information system pa website kapena tengerani fomu pakhomo pa campus. Lipirani K250 ku ZANACO.
- **Bemba:** Lembesheni pa student information system pa website nelyo tateni fomu pa campus. Lipileni K250 ku ZANACO.
- **Requirements:** Credits in English, Math, and Science.

## 3. Kwame Nkrumah University (KNU)
- **Website:** www.nkrumah.edu.zm
- **Nyanja:** Lembetsani pa portal pa website kapena tumizani fomu pa email (admissions@nkrumah.edu.zm). Lipirani K100.
- **Bemba:** Lembesheni pa portal pa website nelyo tumisheni fomu pa email (admissions@nkrumah.edu.zm). Lipileni K100.

## 4. Mukuba University
- **Website:** www.mukuba.edu.zm
- **Nyanja:** Tsitsani fomu pa internet ndipo perekani pamanja ku Registrar ku Itimpi, Kitwe. Lipirani K150.
- **Bemba:** Tenteni fomu pa internet kabili luteni mu itume ku Registrar ku Itimpi, Kitwe. Lipileni K150.

## 5. Chalimbana University (CHAU)
- **Website:** www.chau.ac.zm
- **Nyanja:** Lembetsani pa portal kapena funsani fomu pa WhatsApp (+260776226737). Lipirani K150.
- **Bemba:** Lembesheni pa portal nelyo fwayeni fomu pa WhatsApp (+260776226737). Lipileni K150.

## Higher Education Loans and Scholarships Board (HELSB)
- **Nyanja:** Mukatenga kalata yolowa sukulu, funsirani ngongole ya boma pa www.helsb.gov.zm.
- **Bemba:** Nga mwapoka kalata ya kwingila sukulu, fwayeni ngongole ya buteko pa www.helsb.gov.zm.
