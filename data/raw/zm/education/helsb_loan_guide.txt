# HELSB Zambia - Student Loan Application Guide (2025/2026)

This guide explains how to apply for a government student loan through the Higher Education Loans and Scholarships Board (HELSB).

## 1. Eligibility (Ndani angapemphe / Aba kulembesha)
- **Nationality:** Must be a Zambian citizen with a Green NRC.
- **Age:** Must be 26 years old or younger.
- **Admission:** Must have an admission letter from a supported public university (UNZA, CBU, MU, etc.).
- **Nyanja:** Muyenera kukhala nzika ya Zambia, waka zaka 26 kapena kucheperapo, ndipo muli ndi kalata yolowa sukulu ya boma.
- **Bemba:** Mufwile ukuba umwikala calo wa Zambia, uwa myaka 26 nelyo ukucepako, kabili mufwile ukukwata kalata ya kwingila isukulu lya buteko.

## 2. Required Documents (Zofunika / Ififwaikwa)
- Formal Application Letter to the CEO.
- Photocopy of Admission Letter.
- Certified Grade 12 Results.
- Certified NRC of applicant and parents.
- Two passport-size photos.
- Proof of need (Death certificates if orphan, recommendation letters).
- **Nyanja:** Kalata yopempha, kalata yolowa sukulu, ma results a Grade 12, NRC yanu ndi ya makolo, ndi zikalata zoonetsa kuti muli ndi thandizo lochepa.
- **Bemba:** Kalata ya kulomba, kalata ya kwingila isukulu, ifya Grade 12, NRC yenu ne ya bafyashi, ne fipe fya kulangisha ukuti tamwakwata ifya kulipila.

## 3. How to Apply Online
1. Visit the portal at **www.helsb.gov.zm**.
2. Create an account using your NRC number.
3. Upload all required documents as a **single PDF**.
4. Pay the **K100 application fee** via Mobile Money.
5. **Nyanja:** Pitani pa www.helsb.gov.zm, pangani akaunti, ikani zikalata zanu mu PDF imodzi, ndipo lipirani K100 ndi Mobile Money.
6. **Bemba:** Kabiyeni pa www.helsb.gov.zm, pangani akaunti, bikenimo ifipe fyenu fyonse mu PDF imo, kabili lipileni K100 pa Mobile Money.

Check the official HELSB website or Facebook page for specific institution deadlines (usually between May and October).
