# Zambia General Information & Emergency Contacts

## 1. Public Holidays
- New Year's Day: January 1
- International Women's Day: March 8
- Youth Day: March 12
- Easter: Variable (Friday/Monday)
- Kenneth Kaunda Day: April 28
- Labour Day: May 1
- African Freedom Day: May 25
- Heroes' Day & Unity Day: First Monday/Tuesday of July
- Farmers' Day: First Monday of August
- Independence Day: October 24
- Christmas Day: December 25
- National Day of Prayer and Christian Heritage: December 29

Note: The former National Day of Prayer on October 18 was cancelled and its statutory instrument revoked; it has been merged into the December 29 National Day of Prayer and Christian Heritage (Statutory Instrument No. 173 of 2026). The Government may also declare one-off holidays, such as election or inauguration days.

## 2. Emergency Contact Numbers
- **Police:** 991
- **Fire Brigade:** 993
- **Ambulance:** 992
- **International emergency number:** 112
- **Toll-Free Health Line:** 909
- **ZESCO Emergency:** 0211 363636 (main line) or 322 (toll free)

## 3. General Facts
- **Currency:** Zambian Kwacha (ZMW)
- **Official Language:** English
- **Main Local Languages:** Bemba, Nyanja, Lozi, Tonga, Kaonde, Luvale, Lunda.
- **Time Zone:** Central Africa Time (CAT) - UTC+2
