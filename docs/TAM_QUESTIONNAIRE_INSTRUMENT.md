## Participant Information & Informed Consent

Dear Participant,

You are invited to participate in a research study evaluating **Zedchatbot**, an AI-powered community information assistant designed to provide Zambian citizens with instant, accurate access to public service information (NRC, Passports, ZRA taxes, ZESCO tariffs, NHIMA healthcare, and University admissions) in **English, Bemba, and Nyanja**.

Your participation involves testing the chatbot system and completing this brief 5-minute questionnaire. Your responses are completely anonymous and will be used solely for academic research and evaluation purposes in partial fulfillment of the Bachelor of Computer Science degree at the University of Zambia.

Participation is voluntary, and you may discontinue at any time. Thank you for your valuable contribution.
